    Before you go

    Good to know for the trip.

    Book in this order
    Flights and a hotel with free cancellation now. Then the Vatican 08:00 slot for Sat 19 Dec. Then the Colosseum about 20 Nov (sales open 30 days ahead) and the Pantheon about 21 Nov.
    Carry your ID
    Your Cypriot ID card unlocks the €4 Colosseum and €2 Pantheon prices. It's checked at the gate, and tickets must match your name exactly.
    Getting around
    Mostly on foot, 15–30 minutes between sights. Tap your own contactless card at the metro gates: €1.50 for 100 minutes.
    Eat like a local
    Supplì, pizza al taglio, a trapizzino, roasted chestnuts at Navona. Eat a street or two back from the big squares, and watch for the coperto cover charge.
    Pack for December
    12–14 °C by day and 3–5 °C at night. Bring layers, a waterproof jacket, waterproof shoes for about 15 km a day, a scarf for churches and an EU plug adapter.
    Skip the scams
    Friendship bracelets, costumed "gladiators", Trevi "ticket" touts and unofficial taxis. The official white taxi costs €55 fixed. Keep your phone in a front pocket on Metro A.
